What we pick

A native Swift menu-bar app (macOS 14+) with FluidAudio for speech, ScreenCaptureKit for screenshots and a small herdr socket client for delivery. Everything runs on your Mac; every borrowed piece has a permissive licence.

What could bite

Open questions to settle in the first spike

Latency

The boosted live engine lags about 2 s. If that feels slow, show a faster 160 ms model as a grey preview and replace it with the boosted text. untested pairing

Downloads

About 600 MB for the speech model plus ≈100 MB for the word booster, downloaded on first run.

herdr changes

pane.send_input is a socket method with no CLI command (herdr 0.9.3, protocol 22). Pin the protocol version and check it at start-up.

When to press Enter

Auto-send when you stop, review first in the overlay, or say "send it". This changes the feel of the app, so it's your call (below).

First milestone

Build the risky end first: the pin and the paste

  1. Pin + paste
    Menu-bar picker over herdr's Claude panes; send typed text + a clipboard image to the pinned pane.
  2. Show
    Screenshot hotkey adds numbered images to the outbox in order.
  3. Tell
    FluidAudio live speech in the overlay, screenshots placed by word timing.
  4. Your words
    Boost terms from the pinned repo; replace table.
