Showtell

Open one Claude Code session, then keep talking and taking screenshots while you look at your app. Each message lands in that session, screenshots in the right place, and you never switch to the terminal.

Decision: our own small app Claude Code Local only herdr Stage: study done → pick the tech
The interaction we want

Pick a session once. Keep feeding it.

localvoxtral routes your words to whatever pane is in front, or to the agent that is waiting for you. Showtell does the opposite: you open a session and it stays open while you move between the browser, the simulator and the docs. Every message goes there, in order, even while Claude is still working.

Where things stand

Three questions, three answers

Does it exist?

No. The gap is real.

Popular projects own one part each (Handy 33k★ voice, herdr 43k★ terminal). The few that combine parts are small and none does a persistent target with screenshots.

See the landscape →
Can we deliver?

Yes, through herdr's own socket.

herdr's pane.send_input pastes properly, so screenshot paths become real [Image #N] chips. Messages sent while Claude works queue with images intact.

See the tests →
What do we build with?

Swift + FluidAudio.

Parakeet streaming with custom-word boosting, word timestamps for screenshot placement, ScreenCaptureKit, and a herdr socket client. All local, all permissive licences.

How we got here

The plan changed twice, for good reasons

  • Fork OpenSuperWhisper · first idea
    Your current dictation app. Dropped: it transcribes only after you stop and can't target a session.
  • Extend localvoxtral · after the landscape redo
    Streams, knows repo words, talks to herdr. Dropped after you tried it: it binds to the pane in front (missed your iTerm2 drop-down), needs spoken names to route elsewhere, and is built around "agent needs you".
  • Our own small app · your call, today
    Borrow the best ideas (localvoxtral's herdr client and vocabulary, FluidVoice's preview/final split, VoiceInk's screen capture), add screenshots and the open-session model.
